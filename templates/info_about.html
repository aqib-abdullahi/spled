About Spled

A spatial ledger for physical projects.

Spled is a system of record for roads, buildings, power and agriculture. Teams log dated, located evidence, and a project’s status is backed by that evidence rather than by self-reported claims.

Who it is for

  • Agencies and developers who need one reliable record of what is happening on each site.
  • Owners who monitor works from far away and want to check progress against evidence rather than take it on trust.
  • The public, who can browse a read-only record of the projects that organisations choose to publish.

What guides it

  • Evidence before claims. A status is only as strong as the entries behind it.
  • Nothing is quietly rewritten. Corrections are added, never overwritten, and the reason is recorded.
  • Private by default. Work stays inside the organisation until it is published, and staff names and emails are never shown publicly.
  • Honest about limits. How evidence works says what the checks prove and what they do not.
