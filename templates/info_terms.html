Terms of use

Draft. This page is a working draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer.

Using Spled

Give accurate details, keep your sign-in details secret, and use two-step verification where it is offered. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

Your content and evidence

Your organisation is responsible for the evidence it logs. Only upload photos and notes you have the right to share, and avoid personal information about other people unless it is needed for the record.

Evidence is permanent

Entries cannot be edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected by adding a new entry with a reason. Please check an entry before you submit it.

Publishing

Only verified organisations can publish projects to the public record. Spled may decline verification, or unpublish a project, where the record appears to be false or misleading.

Acceptable use

  • Do not log false evidence or false locations.
  • Do not try to get around the location checks, rate limits or access controls.
  • Do not collect data from Spled in a way that harms the service or other people.

What Spled does not promise

Public figures reflect what teams have logged. Spled does not inspect the work itself. Liability, governing law and dispute terms will be added after legal review.

Changes and contact

These terms may change. Questions: contact us.
