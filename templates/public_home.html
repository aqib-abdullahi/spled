Verified on site

A check you can’t tick by hand.

An entry is verified on site when its reported location falls inside the project’s boundary, or close to its pin. Spled works this out itself, and the result is locked into the entry’s record.

What it does not prove. The location comes from the device that logged the entry, so a match is a strong signal, not proof that someone was physically there.

Questions

Good to know before you start.

Who can see a project?

Only projects that an organisation has published, and only when Spled has verified that organisation. Everything else stays private to the team. Staff names and email addresses are never shown publicly.

What does “verified on site” mean?

The location reported with an entry was inside the project’s boundary or close to its pin. It is calculated automatically. It is a strong signal, not proof that someone was physically there, because the location comes from the device used to log the entry.

Can an entry be edited or deleted?

No. Entries are append-only. A mistake is corrected by adding a new entry that says what changed and why, so the history stays visible.

Can my team share a project without publishing it?

Yes. Admins, editors and owners can create share links that expire after 7, 30 or 90 days, with an optional password. A link can be revoked at any time and shows only entries that count.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in a phone browser, including logging evidence with photos. Offline capture is not available yet, so you need a connection when you submit an entry.

How does my organisation get verified?

Create an account and set up your organisation. You can work privately straight away. Publishing to the public record unlocks once Spled has checked your organisation. Contact us with any questions.
