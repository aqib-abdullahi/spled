Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Contact us.

Who can see a project?

Only projects an organisation has published, and only once Spled has verified it. Staff names and emails are never shown.

What does “verified on site” mean?

The entry’s reported location was inside the project boundary or near its pin. It is a strong signal, not proof someone was there.

Can an entry be edited or deleted?

No. Mistakes are corrected with a new entry that says what changed and why.

Can I share a project without publishing it?

Yes. Admins, editors and owners can create expiring share links, with an optional password, and revoke them any time.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in a phone browser, including photos. Offline capture is not available yet.
